FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The Android app may show short ads and has an optional button to watch an ad for bonus cash.

Is the APK safe?

It is built automatically from the game's public code on GitHub. Android shows an "unknown source" warning for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store.

Does it work offline?

The Android app works offline. In the browser, it needs a connection the first time you load it.

Where is my save stored?

On your own device, in the app or browser. Clearing the browser's site data deletes a browser save.

iPhone?

Open the browser version in Safari, then Share and Add to Home Screen.
